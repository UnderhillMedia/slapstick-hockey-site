1. Who we are

Underhill Media LLC, an Alabama limited liability company based in Huntsville, Alabama ("we," "us," or "our"), operates the website at slapstickhockey.com, the Slapstick Hockey brand, and our live broadcast production application (the "App").

Slapstick Hockey is a brand operated by Underhill Media LLC. Any agreement you enter into with Slapstick Hockey is an agreement with Underhill Media LLC.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what control you have over it. It covers our public website and the App.

It does not cover third-party services you may reach from our site or use alongside the App — for example YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

3. Two different kinds of data, two different roles

This is the most important section for understanding how the App works, so we have put it near the top rather than burying it.

The App handles two categories of personal information, and our responsibilities differ for each:

Account information — we decide how this is used

When a broadcast crew member creates an account, we collect and control that information directly. We decide what to collect and why, and this policy governs it.

Roster and game information — the customer decides how this is used

When an organization uses the App, its staff enter information about players, teams, and games. That information belongs to the organization, not to us. We store and process it on the organization's behalf and under its instructions. We do not sell it, mine it, or use it to build our own products.

If you are a player, parent, guardian, or coach and you want to know what information a particular organization holds about you, or you want it corrected or deleted, contact that organization first — they control it. If you cannot reach them, contact us at the address in section 14 and we will help route the request.

b. Information organizations enter about players and teams

Customers use the App to prepare broadcast material. The App stores the following about players:

  • Name and jersey number
  • Phonetic pronunciation of the name
  • Position and shot handedness
  • Height, weight, and hometown
  • Season statistics
  • Free-text notes ("factoids") written by the broadcast crew

And the following about teams: name, colour, logo, win/loss/tie record, goals for and against, coach names, and public social media handles.

We flag the height, weight, and hometown fields specifically because, combined with a name, they describe a person physically and geographically. Please see section 8 on minors.

c. Information about games and production

Tournament name, participating teams, scheduled times, and the scripts and timing of production cues, together with which user created or completed each one.

d. Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records IP addresses, timestamps, and requested pages as part of operating and securing the service.
  • Error reports. Error monitoring is built into the App but is not currently switched on. If we enable it, a crash may transmit a technical report including the page involved, browser and device type, and the signed-in user's identifier. We will update this policy before that happens.
  • Analytics on our website. slapstickhockey.com uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and move through the site. It sets cookies in your browser and collects your IP address (which Google truncates), pages viewed, approximate location derived from that address, referring site, and device and browser type. We use it in aggregate to see which pages are working, not to identify individual visitors.

You can opt out of Google Analytics on every site you visit using Google's browser opt-out add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them does not affect your ability to use the site or the App.

The App itself carries no analytics or advertising cookies. It sets only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

5. YouTube live chat

The App can display the live chat from a YouTube broadcast so the crew can read messages on air. To do this we use the YouTube Data API.

When this feature is in use, our server retrieves publicly visible chat messages for the broadcast — including each author's display name, public profile image, verified or moderator status, message text, and any Super Chat amount — and holds a rolling buffer of roughly the most recent 200 messages so the crew can catch up. This buffer is temporary operational cache, not a permanent archive, and it is replaced as new messages arrive.

We do not post to YouTube on your behalf, and we do not link YouTube chat authors to accounts in the App.

By using this feature you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to Google's handling of your data.

6. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the website and the App
  • Authenticate users and enforce role-based permissions
  • Synchronise stat sheets and production cues between devices in real time
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide support
  • Diagnose faults, monitor reliability, and improve the service
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use roster data to train machine-learning models.

8. Minors

The App is a professional tool for broadcast staff. It is not directed to children, and accounts may only be created by people aged 18 or over.

We recognise, however, that amateur and youth sport means the App may contain information about minors — names, jersey numbers, hometowns, heights, weights, and statistics — entered by a customer organisation.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Where information about a minor is entered into the App, it is provided by the customer organisation, and that organisation is responsible for having whatever consent or authority is required — including from a parent or guardian — before entering it. Our terms of service require them to confirm this.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe information about your child is in the App and should not be, contact the organisation that entered it. You may also contact us at the address in section 14, and we will work with that organisation to have it removed promptly.

9. How long we keep information

  • Account information — for as long as the account is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards to resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.
  • Roster, team, and game information — for as long as the customer organisation keeps it in the App. When they delete it, or when their account is closed, we delete it in the ordinary course.
  • YouTube chat buffer — transient, and continuously overwritten. It is not retained as an archive.
  • Server logs and error reports — for the period set by the relevant provider.

10. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to provide a copy in a portable format. Depending on where you live — for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or the EU and UK — you may have additional rights, including the right to opt out of sale or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

To make a request, email info@underhillmedia.com. We may need to verify your identity. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

If the request concerns roster information held by a customer organisation, we will forward it to them, as they control that data.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed password storage, role-based access controls, and database-level row security that isolates each organisation's data from every other organisation's.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for telling us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

12. If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users and any relevant authorities as required by applicable law, without undue delay.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service develops. We will change the "last updated" date above, and for material changes we will give notice by email or within the App before the change takes effect.
