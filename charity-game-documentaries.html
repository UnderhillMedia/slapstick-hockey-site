Charity Game Films

We Turn Charity Hockey Games Into Films

Every charity game has a story — the cause behind it, the people it honors, the reason players lace up. We capture that story alongside the game itself, and deliver a finished film your foundation can use to fundraise, recruit, and honor the people who made it matter.

How It Works

Three steps. One film.

1

Before the Game

We learn the story — the cause, the people, what this game means. One call, 30 minutes. That conversation shapes everything we shoot.

2

Game Day

Documentary crew alongside game coverage. Interviews, b-roll, the moments between whistles. We're invisible until a moment matters — then we're exactly where we need to be.

3

After the Game

A finished film delivered on your quoted timeline, cut for YouTube, social, and your fundraising page. Ready to use the day it lands in your inbox.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can you also broadcast the game itself?

Yes — our doc + livestream combo is the most popular package. See our Hockey Livestream Production page.

Do you discount for nonprofits?

We build packages that work for foundation budgets, and some costs can be structured as sponsorable line items.

How far out should we book?

6–8 weeks minimum for charity games; flagship events and big dates book earlier.

What's the difference between a mini documentary and regular coverage?

Regular coverage captures the game. A mini documentary captures the story — the why behind the game, the people it honors, the cause it serves. We do interviews, b-roll, and story-driven editing alongside the game footage.

How do foundations use the film?

For fundraising pages, sponsor decks, social media, and next year's promotion. The film keeps working long after the final buzzer.

Have a game with a story?

Tell us about it — we'll put together a package that fits your event and your budget.
