Questions

Before you ask

Who is actually watching this?

Hockey people. Adult rec league players, their families, Alabama hockey fans, and hockey fans generally across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. If your business sells to people in their twenties through their fifties in North Alabama, or to anyone who plays hockey anywhere, this is a good fit. If it does not, we will tell you.

Is the live audience the whole thing?

No, and this is the part most people get backwards. The live stream is one night. The clips run for two months afterward and that is where the majority of the views come from. You are buying the tail, not the ninety minutes.

I do not have a video ad. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most of our sponsors do not. We write a live read, you approve the wording, and one of our announcers says it on air. It usually sounds better than a produced spot anyway.

What do you need from me?

A logo file, ideally a PNG with a transparent background, and a sentence or two about what you want said. That is genuinely it. If all you have is a photo of your sign, send that and we will work with it.

What if my competitor already bought a spot?

They cannot. Every tier includes category exclusivity, so once one insurance agency or one brewery is on the broadcast, that category is closed for the night.

What if I miss the September 9 deadline?

Logos have to be built into the broadcast graphics ahead of time, so after that Wednesday we cannot promise on screen placement. Call us anyway. We cover events across the country all season, including the Rocket City Iron Cup in November, and there is always another broadcast coming.
