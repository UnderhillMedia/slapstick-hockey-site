FAQ

straight answers.

Do you charge travel fees for Nashville shoots? −
No separate travel fees. Travel is built into your quote from the start. Multi-day projects that require overnight stays are quoted upfront — you'll know the full cost before you commit, and it won't change.
How fast is delivery for Nashville projects? +
Real estate videos: 24–48 hours. For brand, event, and commercial work, you get a firm delivery date in your quote — and we hit it. Fast turnaround is part of the deal, not an upsell.
Can you do same-day highlight edits at Nashville events? +
Yes — same-day turnaround is available for tournaments and live events. Mention it when you reach out so we can plan the workflow into your shoot day.
Are you licensed for drone work in Tennessee? +
Yes — licensed for commercial aerial work in all 50 states. Drone is available as an add-on for any Nashville project.
How often do you work in Nashville? +
We work in Nashville regularly and cover Middle Tennessee year-round. We're two hours down I-65 — close enough to be on-site on short notice.
