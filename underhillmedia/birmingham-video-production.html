Do you charge travel fees for Birmingham? −
No separate travel fees. Travel is built into your quote from the start. Multi-day projects that require overnight stays are quoted upfront — you'll know the full cost before you commit, and it won't change.
What's your turnaround time for Birmingham projects? +
Real estate videos: 24–48 hours. For brand, event, and commercial work, you get a firm delivery date in your quote — and we hit it. Fast turnaround is part of the deal, not an upsell.
Do you handle both video and drone on the same shoot? +
Yes — licensed for commercial aerial work in all 50 states. Drone is available as an add-on for any Birmingham project, ground and aerial on the same shoot day.

Yes — court-admissible legal videography for depositions, day-in-the-life films, and scene documentation for attorneys across Alabama. We know what the courtroom requires and deliver files that hold up.
