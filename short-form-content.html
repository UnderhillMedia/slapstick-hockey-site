FAQ

Common questions.

Where do you travel for short form content?

Anywhere in the US and Canada. Travel is built into the quote. Recent shoots in Alabama, Kentucky, Indiana, Missouri, and Colorado.

How fast do we get the clips?

Same day for featured clips. We edit on site and post while the event is still happening, then deliver the full set within the delivery window in your quote.

Who owns the content?

You get full usage rights for your event, team, rink, or brand channels. We keep the right to post it on Slapstick channels unless you ask for an exclusive.

Can you do short form content and a livestream at the same event?

Yes. Most organizers book both. The broadcast gives the clips a story and the clips give the broadcast an audience.

What does it cost?

It depends on days, number of clips, and whether a livestream is included. Tell us about the event and we quote within 24 hours.
